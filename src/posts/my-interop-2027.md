The Firefox team just launched a [website that allows users to rank the Interop 2027 proposals](https://interop-rank.fxdx.dev/) that they care about. And I care about things - for selfish reasons and for work. And that's a challenge. I want to rank things that only benefit me and my blog but I also want to rank things that would make my daily job easier. Or, at least, allow me to delete a lot of code. So I settled with a balance of five for each.

My final ranking:

1. [CSS `random()` function](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/interop/issues/1354)
2. [CSS `corner-shape` property](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/interop/issues/1368)
3. [CSS gap decorations](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/interop/issues/1374)
4. [CSS `border-shape` property](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/interop/issues/1371)
5. [CSS `object-view-box` property](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/interop/issues/1385)
6. [CSS `flex-wrap: balance`](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/interop/issues/1350)
7. [CSS `reading-⁠flow` and `reading-⁠order` properties](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/interop/issues/1349)
8. [Customizable/stylable `<select>`](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/interop/issues/1393)
9. [103 Early Hints preloading](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/interop/issues/1400)
10. [CSS grid lanes (masonry)](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/interop/issues/1445)
